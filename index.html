What is Sign Language?

Sign languages are full-fledged natural languages with distinct grammatical rules, syntax, and cultural heritage. They express meaning using hand gestures, facial expressions, eye gaze, and body posture.

🤝

Why Learning Sign Language Matters

Learning even basic sign language breaks social isolation for Deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals. It fosters inclusivity in workplaces, schools, and public spaces, encouraging a more accessible world for all.

🎓

How SignBridge Helps Beginners

SignBridge provides a clear, structured visual approach. Beginners can explore categorised dictionary cards, practice fingerspelling letters, review step-by-step flashcards, and test recall with instant feedback quizzes.

Regional Sign Language Disclaimer

Sign languages are not universal. Different countries and communities use distinct sign systems. For example, American Sign Language (ASL) and Indian Sign Language (ISL) have different handshapes, fingerspelling systems (one-handed vs two-handed alphabet), and vocabularies. SignBridge clearly labels sample signs so learners understand these rich regional differences.

🖼️ Customization Guide: Replacing Visual Placeholders with Real Images/Videos

SignBridge is engineered with modular vector representations and standard image structure. To replace vector visual placeholders with custom high-resolution photographs or animated GIFs/videos:

  1. Open script.js and locate the SIGNS_DATA array.
  2. Update the imageSrc property of any sign object (e.g. imageSrc: "assets/signs/hello.gif" or URL).
  3. If an image fails or is missing, SignBridge automatically falls back to the clean, scalable vector hand diagram.
